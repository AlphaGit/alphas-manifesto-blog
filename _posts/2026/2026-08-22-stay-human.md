Back in April I put together a presentation (and did an internal chapter discussion) about the contents published by Helen Edwards from [The Artificiality Institute](https://www.artificialityinstitute.org/).

<!--more-->

The contents of the presentation actually come from her Tiktok account, [@stayhuman25](https://www.tiktok.com/@stay_human25), where she's still active.

These contents are about human authorship and will even when adopting and incorporating AI. She doesn't talk about any particular industry, job, or approach. She proposes a universal framework that explains how the merging of human abilities and AI capacities are intertwined.

I found that so interesting that I had to talk about it.

Here's a presentation with those contents summarized, and the full notes for each of those lessons. Not every video might have made it into a lesson, as there were many many of them and I decided to cut down to the most important ones.
